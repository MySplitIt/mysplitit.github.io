Delete your MySplitIt account

MySplitIt, the expense-splitting app for Android and the web.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  • Android app: open MySplitIt, tap the account icon at the top, then Delete account.
  • MySplitIt Web: sign in at mysplitit.github.io/app, open Account, then Delete account.

Google asks you to confirm your account, then everything below is deleted straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

Email phanee.apps@gmail.com from the Google account you use with MySplitIt, with the subject "Delete my MySplitIt account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some data and keep your account

  • A group: long-press it on the home screen and tap the bin. For a shared group you created, choose Delete for everyone; for one someone else created, Leave removes it from your account.
  • An expense or payment: open it and tap the bin.
  • A person in a group: open the group's People tab and tap the bin next to them.
  • Your profile details: Account & sharing → Edit profile to change your name, phone number or family size.

These changes are saved straight away, on every device in the group.

What gets deleted with your account

  • Your profile: name, email, and how many adults, kids and friends come with you.
  • Your phone number, and the entries that let people find you by email or phone number.
  • The shared groups you created, with all their people, expenses and payments, for everyone in them.
  • Your MySplitIt sign-in.

What stays

  • Groups other people created: you're taken out of them, but your name stays in their past expenses (without your email) so their totals stay correct.
  • Groups that were only ever on your phone stay on that phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. They were never sent to us.

We keep nothing else about you. See the privacy policy for details.
