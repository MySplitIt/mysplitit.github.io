How to use

Step-by-step guides

Pick a topic. Each guide shows exactly what to tap.

Install MySplitIt and sign in

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open it. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.
  2. Open MySplitIt. You can use it straight away without an account; groups stay on your phone.
  3. To share groups with friends, tap the account icon at the top and choose Sign in with Google.
  4. Set up your profile: your name, your phone number (required, so friends can find you by number), and who usually comes with you (for example 2 adults including you and 1 kid).

Why a profile? When a friend adds you to a group, your family comes along automatically, and if your family size changes, every group you're in updates.

Create a group

  1. On the Groups tab, tap New group.
  2. Enter a name (for example "Florida trip"), pick a type and the default currency, such as US dollars or Indian rupees.
  3. Add me to this group is ticked, bringing your family from your profile. Turn off Bring my family from my profile to pick different numbers, or untick Add me if you're only organising.
  4. Tap Create. MySplitIt then asks you to add the other people.

You can't create two groups with the same name, so trips never get mixed up.

Add people

  1. In a group, open People and tap Add people.
  2. Choose Create manually to type a name. Tick This is a family to set adults and kids. Works for anyone, even without MySplitIt.
  3. Or choose Add a MySplitIt user and search by their exact Gmail address or phone number.
  4. Check the name and email shown, then tap Add. Their family joins from their profile, and the group is shared with them.

People who already joined but aren't in the list appear at the top as suggestions. Phone numbers match with or without the country code.

Add an expense

  1. In a group, tap Add expense.
  2. Enter the expense name and amount (both required), and an optional description. Change the currency if needed.
  3. Pick who paid. You're picked by default.
  4. Choose Everyone in the group or Only some members, and check the share shown next to each person.
  5. Tap Save.

Equal splits count every person: a family of 3 pays three shares, a single person one.

Adult & kid prices, and tax

  1. In the expense form, choose Adult & kid prices.
  2. Enter the price per adult and per kid. MySplitIt works out the ticket total.
  3. If the bill came to more (sales tax, GST, service charges), enter Total paid, with tax.
  4. The extra is shared in proportion to each family's ticket cost. Each person shows, for example, "$100 + $8 tax".

It works the same in any currency, like ₹3,000 + ₹540 GST on a trip in India. A lower total is shared as a discount the same way.

Change who came, for one expense

  1. In the expense form, each person shows their adults and kids, like "2 adults · 1 kid ✎".
  2. Tap it to change how many of them were part of this expense.
  3. The shares update straight away. Other expenses aren't affected.

Saved expenses keep the counts they were saved with, so old totals never change by surprise.

Share a group and invite friends

  1. Sign in with Google first (account icon on the home screen).
  2. Open the group, tap ⋮ and choose Share with friends.
  3. Invite people by the Gmail address they sign in with, or add them as MySplitIt users from Add people.
  4. They see the group as soon as they sign in. Everyone can add and edit expenses.

The person who shared the group can remove people or Delete for everyone; others can Leave group.

Balances and settling up

  1. Open the group's Balances tab to see who's up and who's down, per currency.
  2. Suggested payments lists the fewest payments that settle everyone.
  3. When someone pays, tap Mark paid (or Record payment) and the balances update for everyone.

The Friends tab

  1. Tap Friends at the bottom of the home screen.
  2. Each person shows what you owe each other across all groups: "owes you", "you owe" or "settled up".
  3. Tap a friend to see the amount in each group, and Settle any of them.

In groups only on your phone, MySplitIt asks once Which one is you? so it knows your share.

Edit or delete groups

  1. Long-press a group on the home screen to select it. Tap more groups to select them too.
  2. Tap ✎ to rename it or change its type and currency (one group at a time).
  3. Tap 🗑 to delete. MySplitIt says first what will happen to shared groups.

Lock the app

  1. Tap the lock icon on the home screen.
  2. Turn on Lock with PIN and choose a 4–6 digit PIN (not your bank or UPI PIN).
  3. Optionally turn on Unlock with fingerprint or face, and choose when MySplitIt locks after you leave it.

Your PIN is stored only as a secure hash on your phone. Wrong guesses make you wait longer each time.

Security & privacy

Your money talk stays yours

No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Here's exactly how your data is handled.

📱 On your phone first

Groups you don't share never leave your phone. Shared groups are also kept in full on every member's phone.

🛡️ Locked-down sharing

Shared groups sync through Google Firebase. Only the Google accounts you invite can read or change a group, enforced on the server, not just in the app.

🔑 Strong app lock

Your PIN is stored only as a salted, slow hash. Fingerprint unlock uses your phone's secure hardware and turns off if fingerprints change.

👤 Minimal sign-in

Google sign-in gives MySplitIt only your name and email. Your phone number is required so friends can find you, is used only for that, and is never shown to others.

🧱 Hardened app

The app is signed with a private key and its code is shrunk and scrambled, with no source names or debug logs inside. The app's source code isn't published.

💾 Encrypted backups only

Android backups include your groups only when the backup is encrypted. Your PIN and lock settings are never backed up.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is MySplitIt free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. You can create groups and split expenses without signing in; they stay on your phone. Sign in with Google only when you want to share a group with friends.

Does it need internet?

Only to share. Everything works offline, including shared groups; changes you make offline sync when you're back online.

Do my friends need the app?

Only if they want to see and add expenses themselves. You can add anyone by name and track their share yourself.

How are families counted?

For equal splits, every adult and kid counts as one person. With adult and kid prices, each family pays for its adults and kids. Friends travelling with someone count as adults.

How is tax shared?

Enter the ticket prices and the total you paid. The difference (sales tax, GST, service charge) is shared in proportion to what each family's tickets cost.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes, any currency: dollars, rupees, euros and more. Each group and each expense can have its own. Balances are kept separately per currency, like "$347.85 + ₹29.67"; MySplitIt never converts with guessed exchange rates.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not as an app yet, but iPhone users can use MySplitIt Web in Safari: sign in with Google and share groups with friends on Android. Sign in with Apple isn't available.

Why do I see a warning when installing?

Android warns about any app installed from outside the Play Store. MySplitIt is signed with its own private key; check the SHA-256 on this page if you want to be sure your file is genuine.

I forgot my PIN. What now?

For security there's no PIN reset. Clear MySplitIt's storage in Android Settings to start over. Shared groups come back when you sign in again; groups only on your phone are deleted.

How do I delete my data?

Delete groups on your phone with a long-press. For shared groups, leave them or delete them for everyone if you created them. To remove your profile, contact us.
